01 · The decision

Where does the next well go, and is it worth drilling? The answer is spread across five systems.

Data in silos

Logs, tops, OSDU records, economics, maps

Each lives with a different team and tool. Every new question starts with an export and a hand-off.

Gaps in the record

Wells drilled without a key curve

A missing sonic log blocks geomechanics and seismic ties. The usual fix is an empirical relation applied by hand.

AI without guardrails

Assistants that cannot see, or ignore, entitlements

Either the model has no access to the governed data, or it has too much and partners see what they should not.

Subsurface Intelligence puts the logs, the geomodel, the economics and the AI on one governed platform, so the decision is one workflow instead of five projects.

04 · The path, and the ask

Prove it on one field with your logs, then reuse the template

Weeks 1–2

Land

Your LAS files, tops and headers for one field into a UC volume; same pipeline, your data.
Weeks 3–4

Score

Retrain the sonic model on your wells; agree the bar it must clear against your current method.
Weeks 5–8

Decide

Voxel model on your horizons; one step-out reviewed by your geoscientists against their own pick.
After

Scale

Next field, next missing curve, partner access under the same policies.
Ask 1 · business

Name one field and the person who owns its next-well decision

Success is a step-out your team agrees is credible, with its value, in weeks instead of a quarter.

Ask 2 · technical

A two-week working session with petrophysics and data

Map your logs, tops and OSDU records to the pipeline, and agree the acceptance bar for the sonic model.
